How to Play in 30 Seconds

1. The RPS Triangle

✊ Rock beats ✌ Scissors
✌ Scissors beats ✋ Paper
✋ Paper beats ✊ Rock

2. Clash or Recoil

Attack simultaneously! Winning attack inflicts full damage & launches the opponent. Ties bounce both fighters away safely with 0 damage.

3. Damage = Knockback

The more damage % you accumulate, the farther attacks launch you! At 100%+, a single clean hit will send you flying.

4. Blast Zones & Lives

Knock opponents off the arena perimeter into blast zones to take a life. Each fighter has 3 lives. Last fighter standing wins!
